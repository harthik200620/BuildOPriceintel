The five questions you'll get

1. "Why not just stay on AWS forever?"

Cheapest inside 5 years, yes — slide 6 shows it. But GPUs pay back in under 2 years at our utilisation, keys and data sit in someone else's jurisdiction, and after FY31 the rent line never stops climbing. We rent where renting wins, own where owning compounds.

2. "If leasing satellites is cheaper, why launch at all?"

Slide 15 says it in bold: owning never beats leasing on ₹/bit. We're buying a layer nobody can price-hike, geo-block or switch off — and it's only ~35% of the programme, gated at FY29 after the demo proves itself. It's insurance, priced like insurance.

3. "Can 12 small satellites really give us internet?"

No — and the deck never claims it. Broadband stays leased (Starlink/OneWeb/Jio-SES). Our 12 birds do telemetry, messaging and store-and-forward for every site, with ~90-minute revisit, continuous via NTN roaming. Honesty here is what makes the rest credible.

4. "What if the rules don't change?"

The plan works under today's rules — captive VSAT CUG, GMPCS route, administrative spectrum at 4% AGR are all live now; Starlink already cleared this wall. Slide 17's six asks make it faster and cheaper, they are not preconditions.

5. "What's the first cheque, tomorrow morning?"

Small: file IN-SPACe + ITU paperwork (lakhs, not crores), stand up the private cloud on rented iron, pilot leased LEO at 10 sites. The first big cheque — the GPU fleet — waits for the FY28 utilisation gate.

Delivery rules

→Never read a slide. Each has ≤30 words of message; your job is the line, the number, next.
→Land every verdict chip. Slides 5–15 each end in a coloured verdict bar — say it verbatim, it's the takeaway.
→Slow down twice: slide 6 (the money chart) and slide 20 (the bill). Everything else moves at 45 seconds.
→Own the honesty. "No broadband from our sats" and "owning costs ₹49 Cr more" are features — they prove the numbers aren't sales numbers.
→Say "est." out loud when quoting cost bands — every figure is an order-of-magnitude planning estimate, and the footnotes carry sources.
✕Don't defend full-own. If pushed to ₹405 Cr, agree it's the destination — then point at the gates: reality unlocks spend, not ambition.
✕Don't promise dates for rules. Policy asks are advocacy; the plan survives without them.
