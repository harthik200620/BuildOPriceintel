Deliberately left out

A filter considered and rejected, with the reason, stops the same debate recurring in two weeks.

  • In stock — Moved to a toggle above the results. Single binary, and the rail slot is worth more to strength or size.
  • Colour — Determined by material — clay is red, AAC is grey-white, concrete is grey. It restates Type and eliminates nothing.
  • Interlocking paver — A real product but a paving item rather than masonry, with its own thickness/shape/traffic-class vocabulary. It belongs in an External Works tree, not here.
  • Water absorption % — A genuine IS 1077 quality property, but no seller in either target city publishes it. A filter over a field that is empty on 90% of listings produces a broken experience — better to leave it out and say so than to ship a dead facet.
  • Seller rating — Held back until there is a real ratings corpus.
