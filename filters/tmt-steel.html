Deliberately left out

A filter considered and rejected, with the reason, stops the same debate recurring in two weeks.

  • In stock — Moved to a toggle above the results. Single binary, and the rail slot is worth more to grade or diameter.
  • Standard (IS 1786) — One value across the entire category — a filter that eliminates nothing. Folded into the page's line of category truth instead.
  • Weight per metre — Fully determined by diameter, so it would split the result set twice for one decision. Printed on the card instead, so a buyer can convert a rod count into a tonnage.
  • Seller rating — Held back until there is a real ratings corpus.
