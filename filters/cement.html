Deliberately left out

A filter considered and rejected, with the reason, stops the same debate recurring in two weeks.

  • In stock — Moved to a toggle above the results rather than a rail block — it is a single binary and the rail slot is worth more to a spec filter. Default on, one click to undo.
  • Colour — Grey in every OPC/PPC/PSC listing; white cement is already its own Type value. One meaningful value is noise.
  • Seller rating — Held back until there is a real ratings corpus. B2B directory listings carry sparse ratings, and a filter that returns nothing makes the marketplace look empty.
  • Discount / % off MRP — Cement strike-through pricing in this market is computed against an inflated reference. A discount facet built on a manufactured number is worse than none.
